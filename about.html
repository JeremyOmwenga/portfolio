About.

Intro

Hi, my name is Jeremy Angwenyi and I work with computers and financial tools. More specifically, I'm interested in tax policy and credit financing frameworks within sub-Saharan Africa. I am completing my BSc. Informatics and Computer Science from Strathmore University this year and proceeding to do ACCA.

Decided to study Computer Science as I have had an innate interest in computing since primary school. I would take apart electronics at home and reverse-engineer them to understand how they work.

Within campus I dived deeper into the software side, focusing on (just to name a few):

  • Networking
  • Advanced Databases
  • Server Administration
  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Computing for Bioinformatics
  • Information Systems Security & Design
  • Mobile & Web Application Development
  • System Analysis & Design
  • Core Math (Linear Algebra, Calculus I–II, Statistics I–II)
  • Machine Learning & applied AI

I became especially engrossed with Machine Learning applications in finance — learning how financial models are created with forecasting flows, risk adjustment, and calibration according to current market needs.

I'm still in the early stages of my career, learning day by day. I document my findings, opinions, and interesting anecdotes through blogs on finance news and cognitive security (linked on the home page). I look forward to connecting and sharing insights — I hope you learn something new from the blogs. There's lots more to come, so stay tuned :)
